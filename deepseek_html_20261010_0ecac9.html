01 · FEATURES

Everything is simulated.
Nothing is scripted.

Atomic structure, N-body gravity, fusion, spallation, decay, and antimatter annihilation. Every atom is a real object.

⚛️
The Full Periodic Table
All 118 elements with real atomic masses, electron shell configurations, and category colours. Hydrogen through Oganesson, fully modelled from a single data table.
118 ELEMENTS
🌀
Barnes-Hut Gravity
Quadtree gravity with quadrupole moments, Plummer softening, and adaptive substepping. Every atom attracts every other atom, and the grid itself bends around heavy elements.
O(N log N)
💥
Stellar Fusion
Real reaction chains: H + H → He, He + He → Be, C + He → O. Every reaction releases or absorbs energy based on distance from iron, with an iron wall that blocks fusion past Z = 26.
IRON WALL
☢️
Radioactive Decay
Alpha and beta-minus decay with per-element half-life timers. Alpha particles fly off as Helium-4 atoms, beta particles as electrons. Neutron-rich fragments cascade down to stability.
ALPHA & BETA
⚡
Spallation
Slam a fast light atom into a heavy one and both shatter. Two fragments fly apart, three to five free neutrons burst outward, and both parents collapse in a cyan shockwave.
FISSION
🌟
Antimatter
Shift-click to place antimatter. Matter and antimatter touching annihilate on contact — a full E = mc² budget split across photons, leptons, neutrons, and heat.
E = mc²
02 · LIVE PHYSICS

Watch the world simulate itself.

  • g
    Barnes-Hut Gravity
    O(N log N) quadtree with quadrupole moments, Plummer softening, and up to 4 adaptive substeps per tick.
  • 🔥
    Heat & Temperature
    Kinetic energy becomes temperature. Hot atoms ionize. Neighbouring atoms exchange heat 10 % per tick.
  • ⚛
    Nine Physics Engines
    Gravity, heat diffusion, neutron capture, spallation, fusion, decay, supernova, antimatter, magnetic field.
  • 🔗
    Chain Tracking
    Recent fusion and decay events are linked with fading lines. Ten or more events within two seconds triggers the Chain Gang achievement.
